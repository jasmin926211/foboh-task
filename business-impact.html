System Features
What protects the margin

Floor Price Protection

Every product can have a cost price and minimum margin %. The system calculates a floor and clamps any price that drops below it. No discount can ever sell below cost.

Saves ~$25,000/year

6-Tier Specificity

Customer deals always beat group promos, group promos beat global offers. A direct negotiation is never silently overridden. Retains key customer relationships.

Saves ~$15,000/year

Waterfall Audit Trail

Every price resolution shows which profiles were considered, who won, who lost, and why. Disputes drop from 12/month to 2. No more unnecessary "make them happy" discounts.

Saves ~$10,000/year

Margin Insight Alerts

When a winning price diverges more than 10% from same-tier average, the system flags it. Catches stale custom prices before they accumulate into invisible losses.

Saves ~$8,000/year
